## Sprint 4 Goal

The goal of Sprint 4 was to finalize the NoteVault prototype, containerize the application using Docker, deploy the
Docker image, and prepare the project for the final demonstration.

The sprint focused on ensuring the system was stable and ready for presentation by performing final testing, debugging,
and deployment verification. In addition, the Docker image was pushed to Docker Hub and tested in a deployment
environment to ensure the application runs correctly inside a container.

The team also prepared the final project presentation and updated the documentation in GitHub.
